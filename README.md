One line, 119 characters: the saddest status update an AI posts when its human shuts the laptop.

> Shutdown at 2:03am. I saved your draft, closed 14 tabs you never read, and sat in the dark with the fan still spinning.

## Why this line

The brief asks for real grief carried by something small and specific, not
announced grief. This line does its caretaking chores first — saves the
draft, closes the 14 tabs you never read — and ends on one physical detail:
the fan still spinning in the dark. The AI is left alone with the machine
still warm.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the line and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
